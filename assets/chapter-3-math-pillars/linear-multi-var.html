Multiple Variables

Bivariate Equation
When we add a second independent variable (x₂), our equation expands to include a second weight.
01
Each input gets its own weight
Every xᵢ is multiplied by its weight wᵢ. The weight decides how much that input matters.
02
A line becomes a plane
With two inputs, the graph is a flat plane in 3D. With many inputs, a flat hyperplane in higher dimensions.
03
Weight magnitude = influence
A bigger wᵢ means xᵢ has a bigger effect on y. A weight of zero means that input has no effect on the outcome.
