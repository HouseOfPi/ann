Stratified Bins
Binary Splits & Averages

Instead of fitting a straight line, a Decision Tree segments the credit score space by finding optimal cutoffs. Here, it splits at Credit Scores of 600 and 700.

This splits applicants into three risk segments:

Poor Credit (< 600) Avg Risk: 83.3
Fair Credit (600 to 700) Avg Risk: 37.5
Good Credit (≥ 700) Avg Risk: 11.3

By predicting the average risk within each segment, the model forms a step function that fits the non-linear thresholds effortlessly.
