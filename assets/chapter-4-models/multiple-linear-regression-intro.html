The Motivation
Think about predicting the price of a house. The price doesn't just depend on one thing.
Property Size x₁ Total living space measured in square feet.
Bedrooms x₂ Number of bedrooms in the home.
Bathrooms x₃ Total count including full and half baths.
Property Age x₄ Years since original construction.
Crime Rating x₅ Neighborhood crime rate index.
Lot Size x₆ Total land area of the lot in acres.
And many more factors xₙ School districts, proximity to transit, garage size, tax rates, renovation history, market conditions…
