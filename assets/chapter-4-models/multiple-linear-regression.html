Why Linear Regression fails here

Linear regression is designed to predict continuous numerical values (like prices). It cannot be used directly to classify categorical outcomes (like whether a customer buys a product or not).

Additionally, the relationship here is non-linear—buyers are clustered in the high-age, high-income region. A straight linear boundary is mathematically incapable of cleanly separating these groups.
